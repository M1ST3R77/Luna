Interaction with Luna
=====================

Luna uses a variety of keywords to identify the context and demand behind each sentence. Here's a list of all possible interactions with Luna :

Greetings
---------

When you use words like **Salut**, **Bonjour**, **Coucou**, or **Hey**, Luna recognizes that you want to start a conversation. She'll respond with a friendly greeting to welcome you.

Thanks
------

If you express your gratitude with words like **Merci**, **beaucoup**, or **gentil**, Luna will respond with polite phrases of thanks.

Goodbye
-------

When you say **Au revoir**, **Bye**, or **Bientôt**, Luna understands that you want to end the conversation and wishes you a good day.

Update
------

By using words like **mise à jour** or **maj**, Luna knows it's time to update its interaction system to ensure you have the latest features.

Options
-------

When you talk about options, such as **debug** or **normal**, Luna understands that you want to adjust its settings to display detailed information about the technical functioning of the response or not.

.. _Stop:

Stop
----

When you use words like **Arrêt** or **Stop**, Luna understands that you want to stop the program completely. She'll respond with a closing message and stop for good.

.. _Break:

Break
-----

On the other hand, if you use words like **Pause** or **Repose-toi**, Luna pauses. It remains inactive until you press the '²' key to restart it. This allows you to temporarily suspend execution without stopping the program completely.

.. _Interaction:

Interaction mode
----------------

By mentioning words like **vocal** or **input**, Luna knows that you want to change the mode of interaction, one for voice recognition while the other is a simple phrase to write.

.. _Audio:

Audio Management
----------------

When you use words like **active** or **désactive** in connection with **audio**, Luna understands that you want to change the state of the audio. She will adjust the settings to activate or deactivate the audio on her responses.

Voice change
------------

When you use phrases like **change de voix**, Luna understands that you want to change the voice you're using. She will adjust the voice settings accordingly.

.. _General Informations:

General informations
--------------------

By asking for the **date**, **time** or **weather**, as well as a **city** or **country**, Luna uses these keywords to provide the information you're looking for in real time.

.. _Search:

Search
------

When you use phrases like **lance une recherche**, Luna understands that you want to perform a search. She'll ask you what you're looking for and give you a simplified answer.

Launching applications
----------------------

By using phrases like **lance une application** or **démarre un exécutable**, Luna knows you want to launch an application. She'll ask you which application you want to use. Only applications in the Desktop folder can be detected and started.
